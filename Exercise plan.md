### What I do
* Running, strength training and mobility work.

### How to pick today's session
* Alternate running and strength; never two strength days in a row.
* If the last session was more than two days ago, start with an easy run.
* Mobility counts as a session on a day with a long meeting block.
* Sunday is a rest day unless I have done fewer than three sessions this week.

### Length and timing
* Runs are 40 minutes, strength is 60, mobility is 20.
* Early afternoon is best; before 10:00 only if the afternoon is full of meetings.
