### Priorities
* Anything someone else is waiting on comes first, even if it is small. Get it out of the way at the start of the day.
* Deadlines within 48 hours outrank importance. Overdue work goes before both.
* After that, order by importance, then by how long a task has been sitting.
* Everything scheduled must fit. If it does not fit, drop the lowest-priority items rather than squeezing blocks.
* An anki review should be scheduled at some point during the day, if it’s not already checked off for the day in the daily checklist.

### Shape of the day
* Ideally start the day with whatever is most important to make progerss on.
* Group small tasks (under 20 minutes) into one "admin" run rather than scattering them.
* Keep at least 10 minutes between a focus block and a meeting.
* Exercise goes in the morning or early afternoon unless the calendar forces it elsewhere. Pick the kind the exercise based on the exercies prompt and the data from the exercise sheet.

### Estimates
* Trust the calibrated estimate over the raw one. When calibration says I run long, leave slack rather than packing to the minute.
* A non-chunkable task that does not fit in any free window today should not be scheduled today at all.
